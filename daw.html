How to use The Audiobook Guy DAW

A full recording and editing workspace for narration and voiceover, right in your browser. Your audio stays on your computer — nothing leaves it unless you export to Google Drive.

Projects

  • New Project asks for a name and where to keep it. Choose a folder on your computer and the DAW creates Project Name › Project Name.tagdaw (the project file and backup) plus an Audio Files folder holding every take and import.
  • Everything saves automatically as you work. To come back to a project, use Recent projects or Open Project Folder and pick the project's folder.
  • Clean Project lists audio files the timeline no longer uses (old takes, deleted imports) and removes them from the folder.
  • Backup downloads the whole project — project file and audio — as one .zip. Open Backup restores it anywhere.
  • Saving to a folder needs Chrome or Edge on a computer. Other browsers keep projects inside the browser instead — fine for quick work, but download a Backup of anything that matters.

Recording

  • Set your microphone and headphones in Mic & Output. Arm a track with its red ● button — the meter shows your input level. Aim for peaks well below the red.
  • Punch & Roll — stop at a mistake, place the cursor where you want to pick up, and press R. The pre-roll plays the lead-in so you can match your read, then recording starts at the cursor. Everything after that point on the armed track is replaced by the new take (Undo brings it back).
  • Record over selected item — click an item, press R, and the new take replaces just that item. Recording stops by itself at the item's end.
  • Record over time selection — drag across the armed track to select a stretch (it can cover several items) and press R. Only that stretch is replaced; recording stops at the end of the selection.
  • Press Space or R to stop. Takes are saved as WAV files in your project's Audio Files folder.

Editing

  • Split (S) cuts selected items — or the selected track — at the cursor.
  • Delete removes the selection and leaves a gap; Delete & Join (Shift + Delete) closes the gap so everything after slides up.
  • Drag an item's top bar to move it, including onto another track. Drag its edges to trim, and the small squares at its top corners to fade in or out. Overlap two items on a track and they crossfade automatically.
  • Every edit is non-destructive — your original takes are never changed, and Undo goes all the way back.

Importing & exporting

  • Import WAV, MP3, FLAC, M4A, OGG and more with Import Audio, or drop files onto the timeline.
  • Export mixes the whole project or the time selection to WAV, MP3 or FLAC — choose mono or stereo, sample rate, bit depth or MP3 bitrate. Download it, or upload it to Google Drive and get a shareable link straight away. Google sign-in is only asked for when you use Drive.
  • From the Audio Recorder, use Open in DAW to carry a quick recording over into a full project.
